# Editorial Review: IAP PRD and Addendum

The PRD helps the project team and downstream UX/architecture/story readers agree on the academic demonstration's scope and acceptance gates. The addendum retains the implementation contract, delivery dependencies and evidence limits. Reader type: humans; baseline: Microsoft Writing Style Guide. Both structure passes used the Strategic/Context (Pyramid) model; prose ran after accepted structure changes.

All six findings below were accepted and applied. They change organization or wording, not product meaning, scope, numeric thresholds or requirement IDs.

| Pass | Original Text | Revised Text | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| structure | PRD: scope/non-goals followed detailed requirements; E0 blocking sentence was near the end | MOVE scope after vision and move the E0 blocking sentence into it | Exposes boundaries and the central dependency before implementation planning; zero word reduction. |
| structure | PRD vision: separate qualifiers about source-derived roles, the job to be done and absent named-persona journeys | CONDENSE into one source-provenance sentence while retaining roles, objective and session | Original section 164 words; revised section 149. Actual reduction 15 words, compared with the review's 20-word estimate. |
| structure | Addendum: E0-unverified sentence followed the gate table | MOVE sentence into the introduction | Makes readiness visible before technical detail; zero word reduction. |
| prose | PRD: policies/settings/budgets/thresholds “remain Q3”, “remain Q4” or “remain Q2/Q4” | They “remain open under” the corresponding question IDs | Clarifies that these are unresolved decisions, not established settings. One finding covers four locations. |
| prose | Addendum: “one worker/active inference” | “one worker and one active inference, with bounded work” | Clarifies both independently applicable limits. |
| prose | Addendum: “scaling fits training only” | “the scaler is fitted on training data only” | Identifies the fitted component and permitted data. |

Exact word-metrics baseline: PRD 1,677 words; addendum 1,000. Structural edits reduced the PRD to 1,662 words and left the addendum at 1,000; final totals include the clarity additions above. No hard word limit was supplied. No requirement or evidence qualification was removed.
